Stamp Collection

Once, I collected stamps. Now, I create them
As a child, collecting postage stamps was one of my favourite hobbies. Each stamp felt like a little piece of the world I could keep.
Today, I revisit that childhood memory through art, creating my own stamp-inspired paintings of places and animals. What was once a hobby has now become a way to reconnect with the joy of my childhood.

Kid Inside Me

A little piece of my childhood, brought back to life
As a child, I used to draw animals in my own unusual way, using continuous looping lines to form their bodies. I simply thought I could draw bodies that way. It was how my 6 year old mind imagined them.
Kid Inside Me is a journey back to the way I saw the ANIMALS as a child. A reminder that even as we grow up, the little artist inside us never truly disappears
